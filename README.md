# Software Acceleration Team at Kennesaw State University

SwAT exists to accelerate the education of computing students at Kennesaw State University. 
We do this in three ways:

- Workshops and knowledge sessions on the skills and tools most CS and software engineering programs skip (or leave until much later), in the spirit of MIT’s Missing Semester.
- Team projects on non-trivial software that students must fully understand, then present and defend in review sessions. This gives students practical work experience: they will use proper version control practices, create and run CI/CD pipelines, and code collaboratively with teammates.
- Early connections between students and industry professionals.

Along the way, students gain more than technical skill: they learn to work in teams, lead, and present their work. We reward students with a bias for action by letting them run the projects and sessions they care about, so they graduate as capable engineers, ready to confidently work and lead in the field.
